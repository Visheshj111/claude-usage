Your Privacy

Here's how we handle your stuff.

Claude Usage Tracker keeps everything right here on your computer.

What we keep track of

  • The number of messages and tokens you use
  • Your chat titles and message counts
  • When you start and stop using Claude

Where it lives

  • Everything stays tucked away in your browser's local storage
  • If you export your data, we just hand you a file—we don't send it anywhere
  • Nothing ever touches a server we own

What we definitely don't do

  • We never collect your actual messages for tracking or analytics
  • We never send your data to a server we operate
  • We don't use creepy trackers or analytics
  • We don't sell or share your info

Prompt Refiner exception

When you explicitly click ✦ Refine, the current draft is sent to Anthropic's official API to produce a suggestion. It is never sent while you type, and you review the result before it replaces anything. Your API key stays in session-only extension storage and is cleared when the browser restarts.

You're in control

  • Use the Delete my data button above to wipe everything out
  • If you uninstall the extension, everything goes with it
  • You can always come back here to read this again

We just look at the webpage to figure out your usage. All the magic happens locally in your browser.
